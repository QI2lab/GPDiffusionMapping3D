SAMPLING REPAIR AUTHORIZATION AND GATES

The user approved the proposed sampling repair plan with "Proceed" after the
uniform/smooth burn-in investigation. This authorizes the staged changes below,
overriding the earlier requirement to freeze adaptation and proposal design.
It does not authorize changes to the likelihood, direct GP prior, covariance,
regularization, interpolation, positivity support, or precision decision.
The original CPU float64 / relative-proposal / legacy-adaptation path remains
available as a reference. Changes are local on feat/3D; uv.lock remains ignored.

1. Independent posterior oracles: one/two inducing values, independently
   simulated 3D displacements, full normalized Gaussian densities plus GP prior,
   high-order quadrature for moments and marginal CDFs. Compare 128/256 point
   quadrature at rtol=1e-7, atol=1e-8. Four independent chains per backend.
   Preserve prescribed seeds CPU101-104 / GPU201-204 and varied initial values.
   Include an interior positive field with one zero inverse coefficient.
   Sampling moments must meet 99% family MC intervals across <=32 checks.
2. Stable warmup: updates of log epsilon from recent 50-sweep acceptance windows
   with gain 2*(1+window_index)^(-0.6), target .30 annealing / .25 temperature one.
   Explicit warmup lengths, then freeze widths for equilibration and production.
   Record rejection reasons, mean proposal scales and accepted displacements.
3. Investigate full 125-value uniform/smooth problems with unchanged data,
   kernel, positivity and recovery target. Pilot is diagnostic, not a pass.
   Full validation uses >=4 chains per backend with different positive GP-shaped
   initial fields, predefined data/sampler seeds, and all monitored quantities.
   Require rank/folded split R-hat <1.01, bulk/tail ESS >=400, mean MCSE below
   5% of posterior SD; physical recovery still <10% RMS in the central cube.
4. If tuning alone fails, symmetric additive inverse-coordinate proposals remove
   the dependence on |alpha| and have zero Hastings correction. Recheck all
   posterior oracles before higher-dimensional runs. If correlations remain,
   evaluate blocked proposals in whitened coordinates with a fixed warmup
   preconditioner, the same exact posterior, and fresh oracle validation.
5. Compare effective samples per second and CPU/GPU agreement only after
   convergence. Confirm using additional predefined seeds/datasets; no seed
   selection or enlarged intervals to manufacture a pass. Elliptical slice
   sampling is a later candidate, not automatically added to the application.

Diagnostics follow Vehtari et al. https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.08008 and Geyer's
initial monotone positive autocorrelation pairs. Two-chain exploratory reports
from before this repair are retained; they are not production validation.
Existing mathematical/physical CPU and required-CUDA suites must still pass.
Stage 5 remains deferred until the repaired sampling is scientifically valid.

If the additive pilot still shows correlation-limited exploration, the blocked
candidate uses blocks of 16 prior-whitened and Fisher-preconditioned coordinates.
The preconditioner is computed once per chain from the initial positive field
and then fixed. In prior coordinates its precision is I+L.T*Fisher*L; it does
not alter the GP covariance, jitter or target. Each block has a symmetric
Gaussian proposal and its own warmup-tuned scale. Blocks are updated sequentially
in randomized order. The identical oracle and convergence requirements apply.
