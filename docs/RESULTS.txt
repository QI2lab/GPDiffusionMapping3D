PRECISION DECISION

The predeclared acceptance-log-ratio error budget is 1e-4. Float32 fails it:
2D stressed error 0.00116543; 100k ordinary/stressed errors 0.0004924/0.00998855;
3D ordinary/stressed errors 0.0001533/0.0442453. Half-sized float32 arrays and
successful physical recovery do not override that numerical failure. Float64
is the validated precision; float32 remains explicitly warned diagnostics.
No mixed-precision scheme, diffusion floors, or larger jitter were introduced.

On smooth irregular-time 3D data with independent sampler seeds 41/42 and
common data seed 41, 5,000 production sweeps after 10,000 annealing and 200
stabilization sweeps failed the original ten-batch comparison at 4/27 central
positions. Estimated minimum ESS was CPU 8.47 / GPU 6.20. A later diagnostic
found maximum rank-normalized split R-hat 1.84, with all 27 positions above
1.01. MC uncertainty is batch-length-sensitive: the nominal comparison alone
does not have established 99% coverage when its batches remain dependent.
Saved evidence: stage4-sampling-5000.json and figures/3d-sampling-5000.png.

1.78485/1.87365 and minimum projected ESS CPU/GPU 6.62/5.88 and 5.86/5.02.
Every chain has at least one coefficient with zero accepted production
updates. Longer fixed-width burn-in did not establish convergence. The
